Remote Office Management

Your whole office,
managed remotely.

Grace Dougan Consulting provides remote office management for small businesses: phone answering, scheduling, supply ordering and travel arrangements, handled by a dedicated team from anywhere. You get the benefits of an office manager without adding a full-time employee.

Who It's For

  • Owners spending hours on calls, calendars and errands instead of the work they do best
  • Small teams that need reliable office support but not a full-time hire
  • Businesses with remote or distributed staff

What's Included

  • Phone answering
  • Scheduling and calendar management
  • Supply ordering
  • Travel arrangements

How It Works

  1. ScopeWe list the tasks you want off your plate.
  2. Set upWe connect to your calendar, phone and accounts and agree how to handle requests.
  3. RunWe handle the day-to-day remotely.
  4. AdjustWe add or change tasks as your needs shift.

Common Questions

What does a remote office manager do?
Handles day-to-day administrative work, such as phones, calendars, supplies and travel, online instead of from a desk in your office.
Can you answer calls for my business?
Yes, phone answering is part of the service.
Is this only for local businesses?
No. Because the work is remote, it works for clients anywhere.
Can I combine this with bookkeeping?
Yes. Many clients use several of our services together.
