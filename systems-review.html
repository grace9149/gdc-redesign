Systems Review

Find the bottlenecks
before they find you.

A systems review is a deep audit of the tools, processes and workflows your business runs on. Grace Dougan Consulting reviews how work actually gets done, then recommends specific ways to streamline operations and remove bottlenecks as you scale.

Who It's For

  • Businesses using many disconnected tools
  • Teams whose processes depend on one person or a tangle of spreadsheets
  • Owners preparing to scale who want to fix the foundations first

What's Included

  • Review of your software, tools and subscriptions
  • Mapping of key processes and workflows
  • Recommendations to streamline operations
  • Identification of bottlenecks and risks

How It Works

  1. InventoryWe list the tools and processes your business depends on.
  2. ObserveWe look at how work actually flows, including the workarounds.
  3. RecommendWe give you prioritized recommendations.
  4. SupportWe can help carry them out, including custom software where it makes sense.

Common Questions

What does a systems review cover?
Your tools, processes and workflows, and how they fit together.
What do I get at the end?
A set of prioritized recommendations to streamline operations.
Will you recommend new software?
Only if it solves a real problem. Sometimes the answer is to simplify what you have.
What if I need something built?
We also build lightweight custom apps to replace overgrown spreadsheets.
