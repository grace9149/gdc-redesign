Remote Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping that keeps
you current.

Grace Dougan Consulting provides remote bookkeeping for small businesses: we keep your books current, produce timely financial reports, and support invoicing and collections so cash flow stays healthy. We are based in Novato, California, and work with clients in Marin County and across the country.

Who It's For

  • Small businesses and nonprofits whose books are behind or only caught up at tax time
  • Owners who want accurate monthly numbers without hiring a full-time bookkeeper
  • Teams moving off spreadsheets or an outgoing bookkeeper who need a clean handoff

What's Included

  • Ongoing categorization and reconciliation of bank, credit card and payment accounts
  • Timely financial reports you can actually read
  • Invoicing and collections support
  • Cleanup of past-due or messy books when needed

How It Works

  1. ReviewWe look at your current books, accounts and software to see where things stand.
  2. Set upWe agree on a schedule, the reports you want, and how we will work with your team.
  3. Keep currentWe handle the recurring bookkeeping remotely and deliver your reports on time.
  4. Check inWe flag issues early and adjust as your business changes.

Common Questions

What does remote bookkeeping mean?
Your bookkeeper works with you online instead of in your office. Bank feeds, shared files and video calls replace paper and in-person visits, so location does not limit who you can work with.
Do you only work with Marin County businesses?
No. We are based in Novato and happy to meet Marin clients in person when it helps, but most of the work is done remotely for clients across the U.S.
Can you clean up books that are behind?
Yes. We start by reviewing where the books stand, then bring them current before moving to ongoing monthly work.
Which software do you use?
We work in QuickBooks and can help you move to QuickBooks Online if you are still on Desktop.
