Signs it is time for a refresh

Most businesses do not need a brand new site. They need the one they have to stop getting in their own way.

  • It looks dated next to competitors you know you are better than.
  • It is hard to use on a phone, where most visitors now arrive.
  • Visitors leave without calling, booking or filling out your form.
  • You answer the same ten questions by email every single week.
  • Updating a page means calling someone or fighting the editor.
  • It is slow, and Google quietly ranks it lower because of it.

Questions

Good to know

Will I lose my Google rankings?

No. I map every old page to its new home with redirects and carry over page titles and descriptions, so a refresh usually helps your rankings rather than hurting them.

How long does a refresh take?

Most refreshes launch in three to six weeks, depending on page count and how quickly feedback comes back. You will get a timeline in writing before we start.

Can I keep my current platform?

Often, yes. We will look at whether your current platform still serves you. If it does not, I will explain why and what the move involves before anything is decided.

Is the AI safe for my customers?

AI features answer only from content you approve, never invent policies or prices, and pass anything uncertain to you. You can review conversations and switch it off whenever you like.

What does it cost to keep running?

Hosting on Cloudflare is typically free or very low cost. AI features have small usage costs that we estimate upfront so there are no surprise bills.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. I handle the technical work and give you a simple walkthrough so you can make everyday updates yourself.
