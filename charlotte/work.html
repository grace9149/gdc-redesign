Local service business

From brochure to booking machine

The problem
A trusted, busy business whose old site buried the phone number and gave visitors no easy way to book.
The refresh
A mobile-first redesign, plain-language services pages, one-tap calling and an online booking request form.
The AI layer
A Q&A assistant that answers hours, service area and pricing questions after hours and captures booking details.

Professional practice

Clear, calm and credible

The problem
Walls of text and a confusing menu made it hard for new clients to see what the practice offered or how to begin.
The refresh
Rewritten, scannable pages, a simple three-step "how to get started" flow and a warm, trustworthy visual style.
The AI layer
Smart intake that gathers the right details up front and sends a tidy summary before the first conversation.

After
Small online shop

Faster pages, easier checkout

The problem
Heavy images and a cluttered layout made the shop slow on phones, and shoppers abandoned before they reached the cart.
The refresh
Optimised images, a cleaner product grid and a streamlined path from product to checkout.
The AI layer
Product description drafting and a shopper assistant that answers sizing, shipping and returns questions.
What I look at

How I judge a good refresh

1

Can visitors tell what you do?

Within five seconds, on a phone, with no scrolling.

2

Is the next step obvious?

One clear action per page: call, book, buy or ask.

3

Does it feel fast and trustworthy?

Quick to load, easy to read and consistent with how you present yourself in person.
