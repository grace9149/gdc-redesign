SMS Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Overview

At Grace Dougan Consulting (“we,” “us,” “our”), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you opt in to receive SMS/text messages from us. It applies to text messaging from Grace Dougan Consulting and supplements our general privacy practices at www.gracedouganconsulting.com.

Information We Collect

When you sign up for our SMS/texting services, we may collect the following information:

  • Phone number: required to send you SMS/text messages.
  • Name: optional, but helps us personalize our messages.
  • Consent records: to document when and how you opted in to our SMS/texting services.
  • Message content: the text messages you send to us and our replies, so we can follow up on your request.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you appointment reminders, document and information requests, status updates on your bookkeeping and accounting work, and replies to your questions, and other related information via SMS/text messages.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities related to our SMS/texting services.
  • Improve our services.

How You Opt In

We only text people who have given us their mobile number and agreed to receive text messages from Grace Dougan Consulting, for example during client onboarding, on a signed service agreement, or by texting us first. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Types and Frequency of Messages

Our text messages may include:

  • Appointment and meeting reminders
  • Requests for documents or information needed for your account
  • Updates on the status of your bookkeeping, accounting, or other services
  • Replies to questions you send us

Message frequency varies depending on your account and requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may only share your information with:

  • Service providers: third-party vendors, such as our telephone and messaging carriers, who assist us in operating our SMS/texting services and conducting our business, and who are required to protect your information.
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale of company assets, or other business transaction.

Your Choices

You may opt out of receiving SMS/text messages from us at any time by:

  • Texting STOP to the number from which you received the message. You will receive one confirmation message, and no further texts after that.
  • Contacting us at (415) 878-5550 or contact@gracedouganconsulting.com.

For help, text HELP to the number from which you received the message, or contact us using the details below.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

Our SMS/texting services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
