Fractional CFO

Strategic financial leadership,

A fractional CFO gives your business senior financial leadership on a part-time basis. What's Included

  • Internal controls and financial processes

Common Questions

What is a fractional CFO?
A part-time CFO who provides strategic financial leadership such as forecasting, reporting and planning, without the cost of a full-time executive.
How is a fractional CFO different from a bookkeeper or an accountant?
A bookkeeper keeps the records current and an accountant handles tax and compliance. A CFO uses those numbers to plan, forecast and guide decisions.
Do I need clean books first?
Reliable books make CFO work far more useful. If yours need attention, we can pair this with our bookkeeping service.
Do you work with nonprofits?
Yes. Board reporting and grant-aware financial planning are part of the work.
