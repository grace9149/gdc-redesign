Business Strategy

A plan you can
actually run.

Grace Dougan Consulting offers business strategy consulting for small businesses: sessions to build, review and strengthen your business plan, including budgeting, cash flow analysis and growth planning. The aim is a practical plan that fits how your business really operates.

Who It's For

  • Owners preparing to grow, hire or change direction
  • Businesses with tight or unpredictable cash flow
  • Founders who have never written a plan, or have one that is out of date

What's Included

  • Business plan building and review
  • Budgeting
  • Cash flow analysis
  • Growth planning

How It Works

  1. DiscussWe talk through where the business is and where you want it to go.
  2. AnalyzeWe review your numbers, budget and cash flow.
  3. PlanWe turn that into priorities and next steps.
  4. Follow upWe check progress and update the plan.

Common Questions

What is included in a strategy session?
A working conversation about your goals, a review of your numbers, and a set of clear next steps.
Can you help with cash flow problems?
Yes. Cash flow analysis is a core part of the work.
Do I need an existing business plan?
No. We can build one with you or review and update the one you have.
Is this one-time or ongoing?
Either. Some clients want a single review; others keep regular sessions.
