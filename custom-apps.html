Custom Apps

From Spreadsheet
to Software.

Most businesses run their most important work out of a spreadsheet that's outgrown itself — broken formulas, version confusion, no way for the right people to see the right thing at the right time. We build the lightweight custom app that replaces it.

What We Build

If your team's tracker, tool, or process lives in a spreadsheet — budgets, memberships, grants, project pipelines, client records — we can turn it into a real web app: one place to enter data, one source of truth, built around how your team actually works instead of forcing your workflow into rows and columns.

Every app is customized for you — we start with a strong base and make it work best for your organization.

Custom apps pair naturally with our core bookkeeping and back-office services — many clients start with one and add the other once they see the gap it closes.

How It Works

  1. Audit We look at the spreadsheet (or spreadsheets) you're running on today and map what it's actually being used for.
  2. Design We design a simple, purpose-built app around that workflow — the fields, views, and roles your team needs, nothing more.
  3. Build & Launch We build and deploy the app on secure, modern infrastructure, and migrate your existing data over.
  4. Train & Support We train your team, then stay on to support the app and add features as your business changes.

Is This a Fit?

Good Signs

  • A spreadsheet that multiple people edit and it keeps breaking
  • You've outgrown "just add another tab"
  • You want clients or staff to see a version without editing the source
  • You're paying for software that's mostly overkill for what you need
  • You want to combine multiple softwares into one app
