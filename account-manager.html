Open Role

Senior Accounting Manager

Overview

Grace Dougan Consulting provides bookkeeping and remote office management for nonprofits, grant-funded organizations, and other clients across a range of industries. We’re hiring a Senior Accounting Manager to oversee our bookkeepers’ work, review it, and manage the more complex accounting.

This isn’t a routine bookkeeping job. Every client runs differently, so you’ll be learning new systems and handling new requests regularly. If you like problem solving, care about getting the details right, and know your debits and credits, we’d like to hear from you.

What You’ll Do

  • Oversee and review our bookkeepers’ work across multiple clients and projects
  • Manage month-end close, including accruals, and prepare account schedules and reconciliations (prepaid expenses, other assets, accrued liabilities, other liabilities)
  • Handle complex accrual-basis and fund accounting, including restricted and unrestricted funds
  • Prepare financial statements and reports for clients and boards
  • Do cash flow analysis, forecasting, and budget-to-actual reporting
  • Prepare grant billing and reimbursement requests and support grant compliance reporting
  • Train bookkeepers and help onboard new clients

What Makes This Role Different

  • Project or grant accounting background (consulting, research, or a grant-funded nonprofit)
  • Detailed contract review
  • Multi-client experience
  • Working knowledge of AI tools, Harvest, Airtable, Google Sheets, Slack, and ERP platforms
  • Ownership of the work, without needing a checklist

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance
  • 5+ years of accounting experience, including nonprofit or grant work
  • Expert in QuickBooks Online and Excel
  • Strong grasp of accrual accounting, GAAP, and nonprofit fund accounting
  • Analytical skills for financial auditing and knowledge of regulatory reporting standards
  • Reliable, self-directed, and positive
  • CPA or Enrolled Agent preferred

We’re not a CPA firm and we don’t file tax returns. This role is remote, with hours set by agreement.
